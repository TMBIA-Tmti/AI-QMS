## 1. General Documents and Procedures

These foundational documents describe the creation and operation of MDSAP.

| Document ID | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **MDSAP P0001** | Functional Statement | Defines the core purpose and strategic direction of MDSAP. Vision: establish a unified audit program enabling a single regulatory audit to fulfill requirements of multiple jurisdictions. Emphasizes efficient, effective, and sustainable oversight of medical device manufacturers' QMS. |
| **MDSAP P0003** | Roles and Responsibilities | Defines roles, authorities, and responsibilities within MDSAP. Covers Affiliate Members, Official Observers, RAC Members, Lead Project Managers, and procedures for membership modification. |

## 9. MDSAP Audit Model - Seven Process Areas

The MDSAP audit follows a process-based approach covering seven key areas. These are the core elements assessed during an MDSAP audit:

### Process 1: Management

- Quality policies and objectives
- Management review records
- Roles, responsibilities, and authorities
- Regulatory awareness by top management
- Resource management

### Process 2: Device Marketing Authorization and Facility Registration

- Regulatory submissions and approvals
- Product classification and listing
- Facility registration with competent national authorities
- Compliance with country-specific registration requirements

### Process 3: Measurement, Analysis, and Improvement

- Internal audit programs covering MDSAP scope
- CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Actions)
- Continuous improvement processes
- Data analysis and trending
- Monitoring and measurement of processes and products

### Process 4: Medical Device Adverse Events and Advisory Notices Reporting

- Vigilance and post-market reporting
- Adverse event management procedures
- Advisory notices / field safety corrective actions
- Country-specific reporting requirements (MDR, MAUDE, etc.)

### Process 5: Design and Development

- Design and development planning
- Design inputs and outputs
- Design review, verification, and validation
- Design transfer
- Design change control

### Process 6: Production and Service Controls

- Production process controls
- Process validation
- Equipment maintenance and monitoring
- Cleanliness and contamination control
- Servicing activities

### Process 7: Purchasing

- Supplier management and evaluation
- Purchasing data and specifications
- Verification of purchased products
- Outsourced process controls

## Document Naming Convention

MDSAP documents follow a structured naming convention:

| Prefix | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Procedure |
| **F** | Form |
| **G** | Guideline |
| **AU** | Audit |
| **AS** | Assessment |
| **QMS** | Quality Management System |

**Format**: `MDSAP [Category] [Type][Number].[Version]`

Example: `MDSAP AU P0002.010` = MDSAP Audit Procedure #0002, Version 010
